Pizza Service, screen by screen

Every screen of the Pizza Service PWA for our community service crew: the weekly gospel and questions, prayers, neighbors, and the Costco pizza split. Captured at iPhone width from the real app components. Scroll inside a phone to see the whole page, or tap it to open it full size.

  • 390 pt wide, 2× resolution
  • Sample crew and neighbors (made-up names)
  • Gospel text: World English Bible (public domain)
  • Numbers come from the app's own split and fee math
  • SF skyline, fog, cheese drips and pepperoni on every screen
  • "Thank You, God Bless your Service" whenever someone pays or chips in

This week of service

The home screen. Admins post the gospel, a message to share, and two questions; everyone gets an email. Volunteers tap a question to write down what a neighbor shared.

Prayers and neighbors

Prayer requests for someone by name or anonymously, and a page for each neighbor so we can remember them week to week.

Pizza for service day

The Costco pizza split, now on its own tab. The pie re-cuts itself live as people chip in.

Getting in and running the crew

Google sign-in, an invite link for new volunteers, and admin settings.
